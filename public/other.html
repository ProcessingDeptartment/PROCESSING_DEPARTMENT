Monitoring Logs covers Chiller/Incubator temperature, Dry & Grading Room temp/humidity, pH Verification, Thermometer Verification & Correction Factors, and Water Monitoring. Live Intake & Handling covers Abalone Receiving through Washing/Scrubbing (REC 7.1.x). Canning covers packing, filling, retorting, Double Seam, sauce/brine and QC/stock (REC 7.2.x / 7.3.x). Dry Processing covers cooking, drying, grading and dry stock (REC 7.4.x). Live Product covers live packing, bag quality, weight checks and returns (REC 7.5.x). Cleaning covers cleaning inspections, facility/pest inspections, staff hygiene and allergen verification (REC 7.6.x). PPE & Utensils covers equipment/glass inspections and utensil/PPE issue registers (REC 7.7.x). Incoming & Equipment covers chiller batch control, incoming goods, dispatch checklists, and equipment/maintenance (REC 7.8.x) — this completes the 7.x series. More sections (8.x Quality/HR/Traceability) are added as they're digitised — see the Master Record Index for every record and its current revision.
